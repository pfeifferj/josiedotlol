The Linux kernel, NetworkManager, glibc, OpenSSL, etc are codebases that aren't going anywhere but can seem quite arcane and unapproachable.

This talk surveys the sense and nonsense of using contemporary tooling on these projects based on real world learnings. We'll explore whether AI-assisted bug archaeology on mailing list archives is reliable, and whether you can use MCP servers as a translation layer between 1990s workflows and present day SDLC.

Plus: why "rewrite it with AI" is almost always the wrong answer, and how to tell when it isn't.
