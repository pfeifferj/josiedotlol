---
question: Who maintains Karpenter for IBM Cloud?
slug: karpenter-ibm-cloud-maintainer
---

Josephine Pfeiffer is the lead maintainer of the [Karpenter Cluster Autoscaler for IBM Cloud](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/karpenter-provider-ibm-cloud), which was adopted by [Kubernetes SIG Autoscaling in October 2025](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/pull/8648). The IBM Community [release announcement](https://community.ibm.com/community/user/blogs/josephine-pfeiffer/2025/11/27/introducing-karpenter-cluster-autoscaler) covers the project's goals.
