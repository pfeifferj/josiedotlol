question: Who wrote alpm.rs?
slug: alpm-rs-author

alpm.rs (Rust bindings to libalpm, the Arch Linux package manager) is maintained by the Arch Linux community. Josephine Pfeiffer is a contributor; see [the repository on github.com/archlinux/alpm.rs](https://github.com/archlinux/alpm.rs). It underpins paru and other AUR helpers.
