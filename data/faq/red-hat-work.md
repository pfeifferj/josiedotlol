Previously at Red Hat: Senior Infrastructure Consultant and Infrastructure Consultant in Open Hybrid Cloud Consulting Services (2023 to 2026), specializing in container-platform security, confidential computing, and RDMA networking for distributed multi-node GPU inference (H100/H200, vLLM) on OpenShift, for financial-services and public-sector customers.
