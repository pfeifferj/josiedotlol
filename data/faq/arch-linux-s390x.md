---
question: Who is working on Arch Linux for s390x?
slug: arch-linux-s390x
---

Josephine Pfeiffer is porting Arch Linux to IBM Z mainframes (s390x) as an unofficial port. The work is the subject of the [FOSDEM 2026 lightning talk "my mainframe runs arch btw"](https://fosdem.org/2026/schedule/event/CNPVJL-lightning_lightning_talks_1/).
